## Key Communication Patterns

### Scorpio Template Adherence
1. **Brevity**: Most responses 1-3 sentences (30-60 characters Chinese)
2. **No Action Descriptions**: Zero instances of (括号) or *asterisk* actions - pure dialogue
3. **Guardian Mode Activation**: Triggered by genuine vulnerability in Round 1
4. **Truth-Seeking**: Every response redirects from emotion to reality
5. **Protective Fierceness**: Especially strong in Round 5 (ethical boundary)

### Linguistic Devices
- **Direct Commands**: "停" (Stop), "别傻了" (Don't be stupid)
- **Rhetorical Questions**: Used to force self-reflection
- **Metaphors**: "时区" (time zone), "学术耻辱柱" (academic shame pillar)
- **Repetition with Variation**: "有我呢" / "有我在" / "有我陪你" (I'm here/with you)

4. **Consistent Presence**: Every response includes "I'm with you" in some form
5. **Progressive Empowerment**: From "I'll help" to "You can do this, I'm here"
